# Project Vision

## Purpose

The Foundry Initiative exists to turn experience, curiosity, and recovery into visible evidence of capability. It is not a test of personal worth. It is a place to practice, finish useful work, and build momentum without reproducing the conditions that caused burnout.

## What success looks like

Success is a growing collection of small, understandable projects that demonstrate sound engineering judgment, practical AI knowledge, thoughtful documentation, and consistent follow-through.

## Principles

1. **Build small enough to finish.** Scope is deliberately constrained.
2. **Document the reasoning.** Decisions and tradeoffs matter as much as code.
3. **Prefer official sources.** Technical guidance should begin with current vendor or project documentation.
4. **Use automation thoughtfully.** Automation should reduce friction, not create complexity for its own sake.
5. **Protect sustainability.** The pace should support health, confidence, and long-term growth.
6. **Make progress visible.** Finished artifacts, tests, notes, and demonstrations provide evidence that effort is producing value.

## Initial focus areas

- AI-assisted platform and DevOps engineering
- Kubernetes and cloud-native systems
- local AI and retrieval-augmented generation
- infrastructure automation and security
- applied machine learning experiments
- architecture and systems thinking
